About T.A.S.K.

Serving Those Who Served

T.A.S.K. (Training, Ability, Skills and Knowledge) is a faith-based organization dedicated to ending veteran homelessness. We believe every veteran deserves a safe place to call home and the comprehensive support needed to thrive.

Our services include housing assistance, case management, and supportive care to help veterans on their journey to stability and independence.
